================================================================================
                    SMART PLANNER AI (DAYFRAME PRO)
                           PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================

1. THE CORE PROBLEM:
Modern knowledge workers, freelancers, and neurodivergent individuals waste
excessive cognitive energy and time trying to organize their daily lives across
fragmented tools (Google Calendar, Todoist, Notion, Splitwise, WhatsApp, Notes).
This creates a severe "Setup Tax" paradox: by the time a user finishes manually
configuring databases, picking start/end time pickers, and categorizing tasks,
their mental energy to actually execute deep work is depleted.

2. PROBLEM BREAKDOWN:

A. The "Setup Tax" of Traditional Productivity Tools:
   - Notion requires manual database architecture, tagging, and maintenance.
   - Google Calendar enforces rigid, multi-click modal forms (start time, end
     time, notifications, color tagging) for every single commitment.
   - Todoist & TickTick produce infinite linear lists detached from temporal
     reality (there are only 24 hours in a day).

B. Input Friction & Information Fragmentation:
   - Thoughts, commitments, and expenses occur continuously throughout the day
     in messy, unstructured formats (a WhatsApp voice note, a UPI receipt screenshot,
     a fleeting Hindi/English thought while commuting).
   - Manually translating these into separate calendar entries, to-do items, and
     expense trackers creates high friction, leading to abandoned planning habits.

C. Time-Blindness & ADHD Executive Dysfunction:
   - Linear to-do lists fail to communicate temporal density. Users overcommit
     because lists do not visualize day vs. night or available daylight hours.
   - Neurodivergent thinkers struggle to estimate how long tasks take when viewed
     as abstract checklists rather than spatial time blocks.

D. Financial & Habit Disconnect:
   - Daily spending (food delivery, bills, travel) directly impacts daily stress
     and schedule, yet personal finance tools are completely isolated from
     daily time planners.

3. FORMAL PROBLEM STATEMENT:
"Time-poor professionals and visual thinkers lack a frictionless, zero-setup system
that can autonomously ingest messy multimodal thoughts (voice notes, receipt images,
unstructured Hinglish text) and instantly convert them into an actionable, circadian
24-hour visual schedule, prioritized task backlog, and financial log without manual
data entry."
