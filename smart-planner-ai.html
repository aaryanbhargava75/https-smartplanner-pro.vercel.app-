The 24-Hour Circular Dial

Traditional vertical and linear calendars cause severe time-blindness. The continuous 360-degree circadian dial maps your entire day into four intuitive quadrants: Deep Night (00:00–06:00), Morning Focus (06:00–12:00), Afternoon Execution (12:00–18:00), and Evening Rest (18:00–24:00). A live pulsing sweep hand grounds you in biological circadian reality.

help Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What makes Smart Planner AI different from regular calendar apps? expand_more

Traditional calendar apps like Google Calendar require clicking multiple dropdowns for every single event. Smart Planner flips this into a zero-tax 'Dump & Done' model: dictate a voice note or paste a receipt, and AI automatically routes items into a 24-hour circular schedule, to-do list, and budget without manual date-picking.

How does the 24-hour circular dial help with ADHD and time-blindness? expand_more

Linear lists disconnect tasks from the 24 hours of a day, causing visual thinkers to overcommit. The circular dial represents your continuous day-night cycle in 360 degrees, clearly illustrating remaining daylight and visual whitespace so you never double-book.

Can I record voice notes in Hindi or mixed Hinglish? expand_more

Yes! The AI engine combines Groq Whisper Large v3 for sub-second speech transcription with Groq Llama-3.3-70b fine-tuned for multi-lingual and code-switching inputs (e.g. 'Subah 10 baje sync, client lunch 450 rs').

Is my planner data private and can I work offline? expand_more

Smart Planner is local-first: all data is stored directly in your browser's local cache. There are zero third-party telemetry trackers or ad pixels. Optional cloud backup is secured with Supabase PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS).
